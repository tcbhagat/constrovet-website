Privacy notice

Effective 19 August 2026

Claim Companion is operated by AInnoverse Tech Centre LLP. We use the information you provide only to register you, prepare and deliver your requested Cost-Lock estimate, maintain a limited audit record, prevent misuse, and respond to support or deletion requests.

Information we process

  • Your name, verified email address and consent records.
  • Your insurance policy, treatment advice or prescription, hospital estimate, optional cashless authorization or TPA response, and stated care preferences.
  • The hospital administration email you authorize us to contact.
  • Technical security records such as request time, reference number and delivery status.

How documents are handled

Document text is first read locally in your browser. After you confirm the extracted values and submit the request, the supplied documents and report are stored in access-restricted Google Workspace Drive controlled by AInnoverse Tech Centre LLP. We do not send patient documents or extracted medical information to unpaid public AI services.

Delivery to a hospital

Your report is sent to your verified email. A hospital address must verify the delivery request before it receives the report. You may withdraw hospital delivery before verification by contacting us with your report reference.

Retention

Uploaded policy, medical and quotation files are scheduled for deletion within 30 days after report delivery. Minimal consent, report reference, delivery status and deletion audit records may be retained for up to 12 months. Legal or security requirements may require a longer period, which will be documented.

Service providers

Google Workspace services are used for restricted storage, automated processing, audit records and email delivery. GitHub Pages serves the public website but does not receive uploaded documents through the repository.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion by using the data deletion page. We may ask you to verify your email and report reference before acting.
