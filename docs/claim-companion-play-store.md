## Data safety draft

Data collected to provide the service:

- Personal information: name, email and optional phone only if added later.
- Health information: prescription/treatment advice and stated care preferences.
- Financial information: insurance policy values and hospital cost estimate.
- Files and documents: policy, treatment advice and hospital quotation.
- App activity/security: report reference, consent time and delivery status.

Purpose: account verification, core functionality, fraud prevention, report generation, hospital delivery and user-requested support. Data is encrypted in transit. Uploaded documents are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Users can request deletion at `https://www.constrovet.com/claim-companion/delete-data.html`.

The final Play Data safety form must be checked against the deployed production behavior, Google Workspace configuration and every included SDK before submission.
